## What it is

**Container Apps** runs containers serverlessly (no Kubernetes to manage). **Revisions** are
immutable versions of your app for safe rollout/rollback. **KEDA** scales your app based on
events (queue length, HTTP traffic, etc.) — even to zero.

## Why it's on the exam

Deploying to Container Apps, configuring the environment, managing revisions, and wiring
KEDA scale rules to event sources.
